A little about us

Growing together, one day at a time.

Choosing a school is an important decision for any family. At Oakpark Academy, we want children to feel comfortable, enjoy learning and have the encouragement they need to do their best.

Located in Uthiru, Nairobi, Oakpark Academy welcomes families looking for kindergarten, primary and junior school education. We would be happy to speak with you about your child's needs and how to arrange a visit.
